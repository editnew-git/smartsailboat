[Smart Sail Boat](https://smartsailboat.com) ran an informal live demonstration of a faster way to move field media toward publication. The test began with videos and pictures uploaded from a phone for AI-assisted processing, then moved into a review of recent Smart Sail Boat stories and spoken revision requests.

During the demonstration, the group saw a revised story appear in under two minutes. That timing describes this specific live test, not a guaranteed publishing time. The recording captures the team's enthusiasm for seeing the workflow operate from end to end.

The practical goal is to make it easier to turn material gathered on a phone into a reviewable story while keeping human review and publication approval in the process. This demonstration documents an early workflow test rather than a finished system specification or a general performance claim.

Next steps are to continue testing the process with real project material, review the resulting stories for accuracy and clarity, and refine the handoff from field capture to publication.
