## Testing the workflow

The team discussed a field test combining Zoom calls, SHOKZ headsets, AI-assisted publishing and mobile file storage. The proposed setup is intended to support remote work with limited power and connectivity while reducing disruptive background noise.

A multi-day recording and systems test aboard a sailboat was planned, but the footage does not establish its completion. Before departure, the group also considered a Lightning-to-USB adapter and external storage for moving files off an iPhone. During the meeting, the Smart Sail Boat website and its recent stories were displayed for participants.
