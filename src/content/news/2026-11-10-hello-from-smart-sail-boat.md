# Hello from Smart Sail Boat

Welcome to [Smart Sail Boat](https://smartsailboat.com).

This is a place for clear, practical stories about the vessel and the project around it. Future updates will document milestones, onboard systems, cleanup and refit work, maintenance, voyages, field tests, safety, and lessons learned along the way.

The goal is straightforward: explain what happened, what work was performed, what we learned, and what comes next. Plans will be identified as plans, while completed work will be documented as results.

This first post marks the starting point. More stories will follow as there is public project work to share.

## About Smart Sail Boat

A journal of sailing, vessel projects, cleanup and refits, onboard technology, and lessons learned on the water.
