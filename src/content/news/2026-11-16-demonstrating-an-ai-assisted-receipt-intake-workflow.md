The recording date was not supplied; the date attached to this story is the publication metadata date, not the date of the demonstration.

## What the demonstration shows

In the video, a team member uses a mobile phone to photograph receipts for a workflow connected to Slack and an AI agent called Bookkeeper. The speaker says the agent applies optical character recognition to the uploaded images and works through information on the receipts, including material written in Spanish.

The discussion describes a hybrid recordkeeping process in which uploaded items can later be searched through the agent and Slack. The team also emphasizes confirming that each image has uploaded before removing the local copy from the phone. Timestamps and retained message history are presented as useful parts of the record.

## What the footage does not establish

This is a workflow demonstration, not an independent test of the system. The footage does not verify the accuracy of the extracted information, confirm that every receipt was fully processed, establish accounting completion, or demonstrate audit compliance. Statements in the recording about time savings and audit value are the speakers' assessments, not measured results reported by this story.

The demonstration documents an approach under development: capture the source material, confirm its arrival in the shared workflow, and use the agent and message history to help organize and retrieve records.
