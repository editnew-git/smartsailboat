Cloud cover reduced solar generation aboard the sailboat just as reliable power and internet access were needed for urgent remote technical training.

The backup generator was not operating correctly, so Jess removed its carburetor float bowl and found residue from degraded gasoline, including a clogged jet. The visible repair involved disassembling and cleaning those components with fluid. At the time of recording, the work was still underway rather than confirmed complete.

The sun later emerged and the solar panels began charging, allowing the speaker to return to the laptop and plan training on reflashing an operating system with a USB key. The episode highlights practical preparation: stabilize stored fuel, carry metric and imperial tools, and learn basic carburetor maintenance before it becomes urgent.
