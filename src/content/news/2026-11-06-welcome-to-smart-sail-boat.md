[Smart Sail Boat](https://smartsailboat.com) is a place to follow the practical work,
questions, and lessons that come with a vessel project.

This journal will bring together selected stories about sailing, cleanup and
refits, maintenance, and onboard technology. The aim is to explain the work clearly:
what a project is trying to achieve, what has been tried, and what still needs
attention.

## From preparation to practical lessons

The planned coverage includes the everyday work of preparing a boat alongside
explorations of the systems used aboard it. Technology demonstrations and field
tests will be described as they happen, with a clear distinction between an idea,
a test, and a completed result.

Not every useful story ends with a finished project. Questions, revisions, and
lessons from work in progress can be useful to other sailors and collaborators too.

## Stories, photographs, and video

Written updates will provide context for selected photographs and videos, helping
readers follow both the work and the thinking behind it. Each story will focus on
material that is ready to share publicly.

This is the beginning of a new publication. We look forward to sharing the first
project updates and building a useful record of what we learn along the way.

## About Smart Sail Boat

A journal of sailing, vessel projects, cleanup and refits, onboard technology, and lessons learned on the water.
