The team used a live grocery-store walkthrough in Mexico to stress-test a proposed automated content workflow. The recording mixed price comparisons, provisioning decisions and practical sailboat observations, giving the system a long and varied source file to process.

The planned workflow would place each Zoom recording in the correct client Google Drive folder, pass it to an AI agent, generate a timeline and story, and prepare publication for YouTube and the website. The discussion emphasized removing routine approvals and other manual handoffs while still allowing clients to request amendments afterward.

The walkthrough also documented practical onboard considerations. Square containers could potentially be reused as dinghy bailers, powdered drinks reduce reliance on bottled beverages, and glass was identified as an avoidable hazard aboard a sailboat. The recording was a field test; the fully automated publishing sequence was discussed as the next objective rather than demonstrated as complete.
