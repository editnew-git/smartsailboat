Smart Sail Boat used a grocery run back to the sailboat as source material for training on a new media workflow. The planned system is intended to carry phone recordings through YouTube publishing, blog production, and public distribution with less manual prompting.

The recording also documents everyday provisioning discussions, including grocery prices, regional food differences, and a practical method for rolling a heavy water jug inside a suitcase. Team members were asked to review the revised process before a final no-prompting test planned for later that day. The footage does not establish that the automated workflow or final test was completed.
